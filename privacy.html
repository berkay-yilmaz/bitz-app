Privacy Policy

BitzSupportTürkçe

Effective October 18, 2026. Applies to the Bitz app and its widgets for iPhone and iPad, and to the Bitz web pages.

In short: Bitz has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no server. The developer receives no data from the app. Your game is saved on your device. If you turn on Nearby Friends, nearby Bitz players can see your cat's name, your display name and your cat's look. Apple handles purchases. If you email us, we receive what you send.

Who we are

Bitz is developed and published on the App Store by Berkay Yilmaz, an individual developer, under the studio name Gibplay Game Studio. Contact: info@gibplay.com.

On your device only

  • Your game: your cat (its name and look), your display name, rooms, stars, items, purchases already unlocked, memories, friends, visit memories, blocked cats and settings are saved in one file on your device, shared between the Bitz app and its widgets. Bitz never uploads it.
  • Steps (optional): only if you choose Connect Health, Bitz reads today's step count from Apple Health to celebrate your walking goal and give star rewards. The latest count and the rewards are kept in your save. Bitz never sends step data anywhere, never writes to Health and never uses it for advertising.
  • Battery state: read while Bitz is open, for the cozy charging scenes; the latest reading is kept in your save.
  • Photos (optional): Bitz can add a picture you choose to save to your photo library. It cannot see your photos.
  • Diagnostics: Bitz writes short technical notes (for example which pack was unlocked, or a step of a Nearby visit) to your device's system log. They contain no names. They stay on your device unless you share a device diagnostic report yourself.

Your save leaves your device only when you choose to: if you export a backup file (it contains your whole save, including friends' cat names and display names and today's step count; keep it private), share a picture or a milestone, or when your own device backup (iCloud or a computer) includes app data.

Nearby Friends (optional, off by default)

When you turn it on, and only while Bitz is open, Bitz uses Apple's encrypted Multipeer Connectivity to find other Bitz players physically nearby, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, without any server. Every nearby device that also has Nearby Friends on receives your cat's public card: your cat's name, your display name, your cat's look (coat, markings, eyes), what it is wearing, its growth stage and a code that stays the same for your cat, so friends can recognize it. You choose who to visit; a visit needs a yes on both phones, and visits then share actions such as waves.

Players you meet keep a copy of your public card on their own device as a friend or a visit memory, just as you keep theirs. Bitz never shares your save, stars, purchases, Health data or location, and none of this reaches the developer.

Your controls: use a nickname as your display name; turn Nearby Friends off at any time; remove a friend; Block a cat so it no longer appears and its invitations are declined (Friends tab).

Purchases (Apple)

Optional packs are bought from Apple through the App Store; Apple processes the payment and your Apple Account under Apple's Privacy Policy. Bitz reads Apple's signed record of which packs your Apple Account owns, on your device, to unlock them; Restore Purchases asks the App Store for that record again. The developer sees only the sales reports App Store Connect gives every developer, not who you are.

Information Apple shares with developers

If you agree in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give the developer crash reports and usage statistics about Bitz. Apple decides what these contain; Bitz adds nothing to them.

Email and these web pages

  • Email: if you write to info@gibplay.com, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, and use them only to answer you. Mail to this address is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to the developer's mailbox. We keep messages only as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, and we delete them when you ask.
  • Web pages: the Bitz pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; pages on gibplay.com are also delivered through Cloudflare. Like any website host, these providers receive your IP address and browser details when you open a page, and GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. The Bitz pages use no cookies, analytics, ads or third-party fonts or scripts.

These providers, and Apple, may process information outside your country, including in the United States.

Your choices and rights

You can turn Health access off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Bitz or in Bitz (Settings › Steps & Health), turn Nearby Friends off, and delete everything Bitz saved on your device by deleting the app (with its widgets). Deleting the app does not delete backup files you exported, your device backups, or the copies of your public card that other players' devices keep. The only personal data the developer holds is email you send us: you can ask to see, correct or delete it, and you may also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

Children

Bitz is a general-audience game. The developer does not receive personal information from anyone through the app. Nearby Friends is off by default; if a child uses it, the names they type are shown to nearby players, so a nickname is best. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Yakındaki Arkadaşlar (isteğe bağlı, varsayılan olarak kapalı)

Açtığında ve yalnızca Bitz açıkken Bitz, fiziksel olarak yakındaki diğer Bitz oyuncularını sunucu olmadan, Wi-Fi ya da Bluetooth üzerinden bulmak için Apple'ın şifreli Multipeer Connectivity bağlantısını kullanır. Yakındaki Arkadaşlar'ı açık olan her yakın cihaz kedinin herkese açık kartını alır: kedinin adı, görünen adın, kedinin görünümü (tüy, desen, gözler), giydikleri, büyüme aşaması ve arkadaşlarının onu tanıyabilmesi için kedin için hep aynı kalan bir kod. Kimi ziyaret edeceğini sen seçersin; ziyaret için iki telefonda da onay gerekir, ardından ziyarette el sallama gibi hareketler paylaşılır.

Tanıştığın oyuncular, senin de onlarınkini sakladığın gibi, herkese açık kartının bir kopyasını kendi cihazlarında arkadaş ya da ziyaret anısı olarak saklar. Bitz kaydını, yıldızlarını, satın alımlarını, Sağlık verilerini ya da konumunu asla paylaşmaz ve bunların hiçbiri geliştiriciye ulaşmaz.

Seçeneklerin: görünen ad olarak bir takma ad kullan; Yakındaki Arkadaşlar'ı istediğin zaman kapat; bir arkadaşı kaldır; bir kediyi Engelle, böylece artık görünmez ve davetleri reddedilir (Arkadaşlar sekmesi).
